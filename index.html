How to use this app
Everything the shop does with a bid, from typing in the job to getting paid, in the order you'll do it. Tap a section to open it. Your bid stays exactly as it is while you read.
1 · The 60-second version
  1. Bid Estimate tab: enter the customer, add the glass or items, check the total.
  2. Press Save Bid. The bid now shows on the Dashboard.
  3. Press 🔗 Copy Approval Link and text or email it to the customer, or press ⬇ Save Bid as PDF to send a document. Save first, or the PDF won't carry the approval link.
  4. The customer opens the link, sees the total, and presses Approve Bid Now.
  5. The shop gets an email, the bid turns Accepted on the Dashboard, and a draft invoice is created in Xero automatically. Nothing is sent to the customer from Xero until someone approves that draft.
  6. When the draft is approved in Xero the job moves to Invoiced. When the invoice is paid in full it moves to Done.
2 · Two ways to price a line — read this one

Every line on a bid is priced one of two ways. The app is not only for jobs priced by square foot.

📐 Glass piece

You enter the size and pick the glass. The app multiplies square feet by the glass rate, then adds edge work and fabrication.

Use it for: shower doors, table tops, mirrors, replacement panes: glass cut to a size.

📋 Flat-price item

You type a description and your own price. Nothing is calculated from size, and there is no glass to pick.

Use it for: a pre-built window or door, a manufacturer's unit, a one-off item: anything you quote as one price.

How to add a flat-price item

  1. Under the list of pieces, press the green + Add Flat-Price Item button.
  2. Type the description (for example Andersen 400 Series, 36×48, White).
  3. Type the price. If you enter a Qty greater than 1, the price is each.

Already started a line as a glass piece? Open the line (tap the arrow at its left) and use PRICED BY at the top to switch it. Going to flat price clears that line's glass, edge and fabrication choices, so the app asks first if the line already has work in it. You can switch back at any time.

Heads up: Margin % and Surcharge % (the internal box in the Bid Summary) are added on top of a flat price, just like glass. If you want the customer to pay exactly the number you type, set both to 0, or type a lower price.

What the customer sees: your description and the final total. Customers never see the price of any individual line.

Dimensions on a flat-price line are optional. They're there for reference only. For a to-scale picture of a piece, use the Drawing tab.

3 · Adding glass pieces
  • Type the width and height in inches. Use the fraction box beside each number for the part of an inch. Set the Qty for identical pieces.
  • New pieces copy the glass and edge of the piece above, so you only change what differs.
  • Tap the arrow at the left of a line to open its details. GLASS: pick thickness, flat or insulated, treatment and type. The price per square foot fills in and you can type over it.
  • EDGE: pick an edge finish. The price per inch is editable.
  • FABRICATION: open the section and use + and − on each item (holes, cutouts and so on). The price of each is shown.
  • A ⚠ on a line means no glass price is set. Pick the glass, or type a $/ft² rate.
  • The ⋯ menu on each line has: Flat Price Item, Duplicate and Remove.
  • ≈ Round ft² (top right of the card) rounds each piece up to the next whole square foot.

Quick Add

The Quick Add box adds glass pieces fast. Type the pieces and press Enter or + Add:

22x22, 1/2, clear, tempered

  • Quantity: add qty 2 (or x2) as another comma-separated part.
  • Insulated glass: add the word insulated (or IG).
  • Several pieces at once: put a semicolon between them.
  • Whole inches only. Quick Add ignores fractions, so set them in the line afterward.
  • Quick Add makes glass pieces only. Use the green button for flat-price items.
4 · Hardware, labor, extras and how the price is built
  • Hardware/Extra: press 🔩 Pick hardware to choose an item and finish from your own price list (pictures; the price fills in), or + Type a hardware item to type a name and cost. ✎ Edit price list (owner login) sets the items, their prices and any extra % for a finish. No prices are built in.
  • Labor Costs: a name and a cost. Marked tax free.
  • Additional Costs: a name and either a dollar amount or a percentage of the materials.

Margin and surcharge (internal only)

The Internal Pricing Only box in the Bid Summary is never shown to the customer. It builds the final price like this:

Part of the bidMargin %Surcharge %
Glass, flat-price items, edge, fabrication, additional costsAddedAdded
HardwareAddedNot added
LaborNot addedNot added

No sales tax is added to the total.

Pricing person (Chris / Jessica)

The two chips near the top of Job Info choose who is pricing the job. Jessica's chip doubles the edge and fabrication prices. Glass rates are not affected.

Deposit

The Deposit button in the Bid Summary is on by default: a 50% deposit line shows on the bid, the PDF and the customer's approval page. Turn it off and that line goes away.

5 · Customers and job info
  • At the top of the Bid Estimate tab, type the customer's name, or press + Customer to pick a saved one and fill in their details.
  • The Customers tab is where you add, edit and search saved customers (name, phone, email, address, billing address, company).
  • Add an email. It's where the automatic review request goes after the invoice is paid in full.
  • Leave Billing Address blank unless it's different from the job location.
6 · Notes and exclusions
  • Additional Notes is for things like lead time or what the customer provides.
  • Exclusions is for what the price does not cover (permits, disposal of old glass, electrical by others).
  • Both print on the PDF.
7 · Giving the customer a choice (bid options)

Use options when the customer should choose between versions of the same job, for example tempered or annealed glass.

  1. On the Bid tab, press ＋ Add another option (above the pieces). The new option starts as a copy of the first.
  2. Give each option a Name the customer sees and a one-sentence description of what's different.
  3. Use the option tabs to switch. Everything below (pieces, hardware, labor, extras) belongs to the option you're on, and each tab shows its own total.
  4. Up to 4 options. Use Remove this option to drop one.

The customer's approval page shows the options side by side with the glass list and total (and the deposit, if on), without line prices. They pick one, then confirm. The PDF holds every option. Once a bid is approved, options can't be added or removed.

8 · The Drawing tab

The ✏ Drawing tab makes shop drawings. It is separate from the bid: nothing in it has a price, and nothing in it is added to a bid, the PDF or the customer's approval page. The customer only sees a drawing if you send them the customer version.

Start a drawing

  • Type a Drawing name (required to save), and a customer and job if you want them on the printout.
  • ＋ Add panel adds a piece of glass: Rectangle, Circle / oval, Arch top, or Polygon (any shape, with as many corners as you need).
  • 📋 Copy from a bid brings in the glass pieces you already entered (sizes only, plus any holes and notes from the old sketch). The bid is not changed.

Shower layout (fastest way)

  • Press 🚿 Shower layout, pick the layout (door only, door + panel, panel + door + panel, 90° corner with a return, or a fixed panel), and type the opening width, glass height and door width.
  • The app takes off your gaps (wall, hinge, strike, glass-to-glass, under the door) and draws every piece at its glass size, with hinges, the handle and wall clamps already placed. The opening is dimensioned and the sizes and gaps used are written into the shop notes.
  • Set your own gap and hardware numbers once. The starting numbers are not a supplier spec. Whatever you type is remembered on that computer for next time.
  • Pick the layout from the pictures: door only, door + panel, panel + door + panel, door + notched panel (knee wall), 90° corner with a return, neo-angle, or a fixed panel. Also pick beveled or square hinges, clamps or U-channel for the fixed glass, which way the door opens, and an optional fixed transom.
  • Enter the left and right wall heights. If they differ, the glass is cut to the shorter one and the shop notes flag it as out of plumb.
  • Everything it draws is normal panels and markers, so you can fix anything by hand afterwards.
  • ➜ Send to bid copies the drawing's glass sizes and hardware counts into the bid open on the Bid tab. It asks first, it only adds lines, and the drawing stays separate. Glass type and edge copy from the bid's last piece; hardware takes its price from your price list or is left for you to fill in.
  • ☆ Save as template keeps a drawing you reuse; pick it later from 📁 My templates. Templates are saved on that computer only.

Shape it

  • Click a panel to select it. Its exact numbers show on the right: width, height, arch rise, and each corner set to Square, Round or Cut with a size. Type sizes like 36, 36.5 or 36 1/2.
  • Drag a panel to move it. Drag the yellow square at its corner to resize it. On a polygon, drag the yellow squares to move each corner, or edit the points in the list.
  • Also set the Frame (none, metal, wood), the glass look, and Muntins (columns × rows).

Holes and hardware

  • Pick Hole, Hinge, Handle, Lock, Standoff or Notch in the toolbar, then click inside a panel to place it. Click one to drag it or type its exact size and position. Positions are measured from the panel's top-left corner.
  • Press Esc or ↖ Select when you're done placing.

Dimensions and text

  • Every panel gets its overall width and height automatically on the shop version.
  • ↔ Dimension draws a measurement between any two points (it snaps to panel corners). T Text adds a label.
  • On each dimension, hole and text you choose Shop + customer or Shop version only. New dimensions start as shop only.

Two versions

  • Shop sheet: dimensions, numbered holes, a panel list, a holes-and-hardware schedule and your shop notes. Download as PDF, PNG or SVG.
  • Customer version: a clean picture. No hole numbers, no shop notes and no shop-only items. Overall sizes appear only if you tick Show overall sizes. Use 👁 Customer preview to see it before you send it.

Saving

  • 💾 Save stores the drawing in the app so everyone can open it from the list at the top. The app warns before you open another drawing with unsaved changes.
  • ⬇ Save file / ⬆ Open file keep a copy as a file. ⬇ Backup on the Dashboard includes saved drawings.
  • Handy extras: ↶ ↷ undo and redo (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y), zoom and Fit, and Snap to a grid (1/8" to 6"). Delete removes the selected item and the arrow keys nudge it.
  • Only the owner login can delete a saved drawing.
9 · Saving, the PDF and the approval link
  • Save Bid stores the bid on the Dashboard. To change a saved bid, press Open on the Dashboard, make your changes, and press Save Bid again.
  • 🔗 Copy Approval Link is greyed out until the bid is saved. Send the copied link to the customer.
  • ⬇ Save Bid as PDF makes the customer document: descriptions and the total, with the approval link if the bid was saved first. With options, one PDF covers all of them.
  • ↺ NEW BID (top right) clears the form to start over. It asks first.
  • If you try to leave a bid that has unsaved changes, the app asks before you go.
  • If you change the price of a bid that's already approved, the app warns you: the Xero invoice does not change, so you would have to fix it in Xero yourself.
10 · The Dashboard and what happens after approval

Each job moves through four stages: Bid → Accepted → Invoiced → Done. Use the filter buttons or the search box (customer, job, location or invoice number) to find one.

  • Open loads a bid back into the Bid tab.
  • ✓ Approve Manually is for a customer who agreed in person or by phone. Don't use it for a bid the customer can still approve through the link. It asks you to confirm, asks which option if the bid has options, then creates the Xero invoice right away.
  • A red ⚠ Stage may be stale badge means an invoice was voided in Xero. Check the job, then click the badge to dismiss it.
  • ⬇ Backup downloads every job and customer as a backup file.

Who can do what

Everyone can create and edit bids and approve them. Undo (voids the Xero invoice and reopens the bid), the ✕ delete button and Manage Staff are for the owner login only.

11 · Quick answers
  • How do I log in? Owner: leave the email blank and enter the PIN. Employees: type your email, then the PIN.
  • The total is missing a line. A glass piece with no rate (⚠) adds nothing until you pick the glass or type a rate. A flat-price item adds nothing until you type its price.
  • The link button won't click. Save the bid first.
  • I can't find Undo or Delete. Those are owner-only. Employee logins don't show them.
  • Why does it say draft in Xero? Invoices are created as drafts on purpose. Nothing reaches the customer until someone approves the draft in Xero.
